In a toy example about study methods, prior experience $Z$ influences both the choice of study method $X$ and exam performance $Y$.

```text
Z ──▶ X ──▶ Y
└─────────▶ Y
```

If $Z$ blocks the backdoor paths, outcomes are consistent with the relevant intervention, and both methods are observed in each experience group, the adjusted mean is

$$
\mathbb E[Y\mid\mathrm{do}(X=x)] =
\sum_z \mathbb E[Y\mid X=x,Z=z]\,P(Z=z).
$$

Toy arithmetic. If the two experience groups are equally common and their observed means under method $x$ are 70 and 86, the adjusted mean is 78.
