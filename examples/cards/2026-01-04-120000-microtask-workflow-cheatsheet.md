Quick reference for everyday commands:

- `mt add "text"` — raw inbox capture.
- `mt move "@ref" doing` — start working on a task.
- `mt done "@ref"` — complete a task after confirmation.
- `mt comment "@ref" -m "note"` — append timestamped activity.
- `mt list` — see visible, unarchived cards; `mt list --kind note` for notes, or `mt list --all` for the complete inventory.
