[Jumper et al., Nature (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2) report a substantial improvement in structure prediction accuracy on CASP14 and evaluate pLDDT as a predictor of local structural accuracy.

| Evidence in the paper | Question for our benchmark |
| --- | --- |
| CASP14 provides a blind structure-prediction comparison | Do our target families have similar representation? |
| pLDDT tracks observed local accuracy in the reported analysis | Does agreement hold in our low-confidence bins? |

For our own analysis @391442c8, plot the mean observed accuracy in each confidence bin. For residues $i$ in bin $B$, with observed local scores $y_i$ on a common scale,

$$
\bar y_B = \frac{1}{|B|}\sum_{i\in B} y_i.
$$

Report $|B|$ next to each estimate. This is a proposed diagnostic for our benchmark. Bring the transfer question to journal club @a54c8f1c.
