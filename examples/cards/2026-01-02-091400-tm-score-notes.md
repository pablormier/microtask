[Zhang and Skolnick, Proteins (2004)](https://doi.org/10.1002/prot.20264) introduce TM-score for assessing similarity between predicted and reference structures. The distance scale depends on protein length, and the score weights smaller aligned distances more strongly.

For an aligned pair at distance $d$, the weight has the form

$$
w(d) = \frac{1}{1 + (d/d_0)^2},
$$

where $d_0$ is the length-dependent distance scale. The paper describes the full normalization and alignment procedure.

For the benchmark report, document the alignment convention and the population over which scores are averaged. The aggregation bug @ea22a004 is about that second choice.
